# Portfolio Simulation Disclaimer

**This is a fictional cybersecurity risk assessment portfolio project.**

HarborPoint Financial Services is a simulated mid-market regional bank created for educational purposes. All organizational structure, technology environment, business processes, identified risks, findings, metrics, and recommendations are simulated and do not represent a real company, actual security posture, or real organizations.

---

## Assessment Scope

**Organization:** HarborPoint Financial Services (fictional)  
**Size:** 175 employees; $35M annual revenue; 40,000 customers  
**Geography:** Mid-Atlantic region (Maryland, Virginia, Pennsylvania)  
**Assessment Period:** October 2026 (1 month, simulated)  
**Framework:** NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0

---

## What Is Simulated

- **Technology environment:** All systems, applications, infrastructure, and configurations
- **Identified risks:** All 20 risks, threat scenarios, and control gaps
- **Assessment artifacts:** Scan results, policy reviews, system configurations, interview notes
- **Metrics and dashboards:** All KPIs, KRIs, and risk scores
- **Remediation roadmap:** Prioritization, budget estimates, timeline
- **Evidence:** All 39 simulated evidence items linking to risks

---

## What Is Real

- **Assessment methodology:** Risk scoring approach, NIST maturity assessment methodology, gap analysis
- **Framework:** Official NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 (6 Functions, 22 Subcategories)
- **Risk concepts:** Threat models, control effectiveness logic, residual risk reasoning
- **Industry patterns:** Risk types and priorities reflect actual financial services environments

---

## Limitations

This portfolio demonstrates risk assessment methodology within the following bounds:

1. **Scope:** One-month assessment with no on-site interviews, no penetration testing, no third-party audits
2. **Architecture:** Single-region AWS, single ISP at branch locations, partial SIEM coverage
3. **Maturity:** Initial/Developing (1.8 of 5.0); represents a common stage for growth-stage financial institutions
4. **Evidence:** All artifacts are simulated; no real system configurations, test results, or compliance documentation
5. **Precision:** Risk scores and maturity ratings are project-specific estimates, not calibrated against real organizational data

---

## Intended Use

This portfolio is intended to demonstrate:

- Ability to conduct structured risk assessment using established frameworks
- Understanding of NIST CSF 2.0 outcomes and maturity assessment
- Communication of risk to executive audiences
- Prioritization of remediation initiatives based on business impact
- Integration of multiple security domains (cloud, identity, backup, third-party risk)

This is **not** intended to:

- Represent actual risks at any real organization
- Serve as a production risk assessment or remediation plan
- Be used as a template for risk scoring without organizational calibration
- Substitute for real on-site assessment, threat modeling, or penetration testing

---

**Use this portfolio as an example of risk assessment methodology and executive communication. Adapt the approach to real environments with real data, stakeholder input, and organizational context.**
