================================================================================
ASSESSMENT METHODOLOGY
================================================================================

Risk Scoring:           5×5 Likelihood × Impact matrix (1–25 scale)
Risk Categories:       Critical (16–25), High (8–15), Moderate (4–7), Low (1–3)
Residual Risk:         Assessed via documented judgment (not percentage multipliers)
NIST CSF Framework:    Official 2.0 structure (6 Functions, 22 Subcategories)
Maturity Scale:        Project-specific 0–5 scale (distinct from NIST outcomes)
  0 = Not Implemented
  1 = Initial (ad-hoc, reactive)
  2 = Developing (documented, limited consistency)
  3 = Defined (standardized, repeatable)
  4 = Managed (metrics-driven, continuous improvement)
  5 = Optimized (proactive, integrated)

================================================================================
KEY ASSUMPTIONS & CONSTRAINTS
================================================================================

Assumptions:
  • Assessment scope is simulation (no real data collected)
  • Technology environment is representative of mid-market financial services
  • Risk likelihood and impact reflect financial services threat landscape
  • Residual risk assumes controls are properly implemented
  • Phased timeline assumes 60% Security Manager allocation + existing staff

Constraints:
  • Limited security staffing (0.6 FTE baseline + 0.5 FTE proposed)
  • Budget approval required for Phase 1 ($115K); sequential approval for Phases 2–4
  • Vendor dependencies (secrets platform, PAM) introduce 60–90 day timelines
  • Staff turnover risk during long-duration initiatives (PAM, threat hunting)
  • Single-region AWS architecture limits RTO/RPO targets

Mitigations:
  • Phase 1 focus on quick wins (MFA, 30 days) + foundation (backup, governance)
  • Parallel execution where dependencies allow
  • Quarterly risk acceptance reviews
  • Year 2 planning begins in Q3 2027
